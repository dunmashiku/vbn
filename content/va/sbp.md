## What SBP is

The Survivor Benefit Plan (SBP) is a Department of Defense program, not a VA program. It pays a monthly annuity to an eligible survivor, such as a spouse or dependent children, after a military retiree dies. Retired pay stops when a retiree dies, so SBP is how part of that income can continue for the family.

## When you must decide

You make your SBP election before your retirement date, usually on DD Form 2656 with your retirement or finance office. The decision is hard to change later, so talk it through with your retirement services officer and your spouse well before you retire.

## Spouse consent

If you are married, you are covered at the full level by default. Choosing less than full coverage, child-only coverage or declining SBP requires your spouse's written, notarized consent.

## Cost and benefit basics

Premiums are taken from retired pay. For spouse coverage, the premium is generally 6.5% of the base amount you choose, and the annuity is generally 55% of that base amount. Check current rules and amounts with the official SBP page and your retirement services officer, since details can change.
