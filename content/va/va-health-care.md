## VA health care is separate from TRICARE

VA health care is run by the Department of Veterans Affairs and is separate from TRICARE. Many veterans, including retirees, can use both. Eligibility depends on your service, discharge and other factors; VA explains the rules at https://www.va.gov/health-care/eligibility/. Service members can learn about applying before they leave at https://www.va.gov/health-care/eligibility/active-duty/.

## How to apply

The application is the Application for Health Benefits, VA Form 10-10EZ. You can apply online (https://www.va.gov/forms/10-10ez/apply-va-healthcare/), by phone, by mail to the Health Eligibility Center, PO Box 5207, Janesville, WI 53547-5207, or in person at a VA medical center or clinic (https://www.va.gov/find-locations/).

A VA-accredited representative can help you apply for free (https://www.va.gov/get-help-from-accredited-representative).

## What to have ready

Gather your DD-214 or other separation documents, Social Security numbers for you and your dependents, insurance card information, and details about your service history and any toxic exposures. Income and deductible expense information from the prior year may also be needed if you don't qualify on other grounds.

## After you apply

VA usually decides in less than a week. If it has been longer, call 877-222-8387 (TTY 711) instead of applying again. To update your information later, use VA Form 10-10EZR.
