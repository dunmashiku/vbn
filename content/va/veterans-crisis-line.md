## Get help now

If you are a veteran or service member in crisis, having thoughts of suicide or of ending your life, thinking about self-harm, or feeling hopeless or overwhelmed, or if you are worried about someone who is, contact the Veterans Crisis Line now. It is free, confidential and available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year.

Call: dial 988 then press 1. Text: 838255. Chat online: https://www.veteranscrisisline.net/get-help-now/chat/

If someone is in immediate danger, call 911.

## Who it serves

The Veterans Crisis Line serves veterans, service members, National Guard and Reserve members, and their families and friends. You don't have to be enrolled in VA benefits or health care to use it.

## Outside the United States

Service members can use the Military Crisis Line. On a military base, dial DSN 988. Off base overseas, regional numbers are listed at https://www.veteranscrisisline.net/get-help-now/military-crisis-line/. If one number doesn't connect, try another region's number.
