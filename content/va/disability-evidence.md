## Documents every disability claim uses

Most disability claims rely on three kinds of documents: your DD-214 or other separation documents, your service treatment records, and medical evidence such as doctors' reports, X-rays and test results. You can send these yourself or give VA permission to collect them for you.

Private (civilian) medical records can also support a claim. If you saw a doctor outside the military for a condition, ask that office for copies or authorize VA to request them.

## What an original claim must show

For a first claim on a service-connected condition, the evidence needs to show three things: a current disability, an event, injury or disease during service, and a link between the two. That link is usually shown through medical records or a medical opinion.

Some conditions are presumed to be service connected, so you don't need to prove the link. Examples include certain chronic illnesses that appear within one year of discharge, illnesses linked to toxic exposures, and some illnesses of former prisoners of war.

## Statements from you and others

Lay evidence, written statements from you or people who know you, can support a claim. You can use plain paper, a Lay or Witness Statement (VA Form 21-10210, https://www.va.gov/forms/21-10210) or a Statement in Support of Claim (VA Form 21-4138, https://www.va.gov/forms/21-4138).

## Other claim types and forms

An increased claim needs current evidence that a condition got worse. A secondary claim is for a new condition caused by an existing service-connected disability. A supplemental claim needs new and relevant evidence for a claim VA already denied.

Some claims need extra forms. For example, PTSD and other mental health claims use VA Form 21-0781, and Individual Unemployability uses VA Form 21-8940. The main disability application is VA Form 21-526EZ (https://www.va.gov/forms/21-526ez).

If your records were destroyed in the 1973 National Personnel Records Center fire, VA can help reconstruct them (https://www.va.gov/records/get-military-service-records/reconstruct-records).
