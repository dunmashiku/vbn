## When coverage ends

If you separate from active duty without retiring, your TRICARE eligibility generally ends at 11:59 p.m. on your last day of duty. Separating is different from retiring: retirees can enroll in a retiree TRICARE plan, but people who separate generally can't.

## Transitional coverage (TAMP)

You and your family may get temporary transitional coverage through the Transitional Assistance Management Program (TAMP) if your service grants it (https://www.tricare.mil/tamp). Check with your personnel office whether you qualify and for how long.

After TAMP ends, you may be able to buy temporary coverage through the Continued Health Care Benefit Program (CHCBP) (https://tricare.mil/chcbp).

## Before your last day

Schedule your Separation History and Physical Examination (SHPE) 90 to 180 days before your end date (https://www.health.mil/SHPE). TRICARE Prime continues during terminal leave.

Family members 18 and older can keep access to their records on milConnect for a period after separation.

## Other options

If your spouse is a TRICARE-eligible service member and you are covered as a family member, you may be able to make enrollment changes within 90 days of your separation or the end of TAMP. You can also apply for VA health care, which is a separate program (https://www.va.gov/health-care/how-to-apply/).
