## When to start

The Transition Assistance Program (TAP) starts one year before separation, or two years before retirement. Starting on time gives you room to act on what you learn, such as the disability claim window that closes 90 days before separation.

## The VA portion of TAP

The VA part of TAP is a course called VA Benefits and Services, led by VA Benefits Advisors. It covers disability compensation, health care, education, home loans, life insurance and other benefits. It is offered online through TAPevents.mil (https://tapevents.mil/courses/405). For mandatory TAP credit, register with your CAC or DoD ID and select Core Requirements.

VA also publishes a "Know Before You Go" workbook to help you prepare.

## Optional VA courses

Optional TAP modules go deeper on single topics, including Disability Compensation (https://tapevents.mil/courses/412), VA Education Benefits (https://tapevents.mil/courses/407), VA Home Loan Guaranty, VA Life Insurance (https://tapevents.mil/courses/418) and Vet Centers.

## Getting help

You can find a VA TAP Benefits Advisor through VA's transition programs site (https://discover.va.gov/transition-programs/va-transition-assistance-program-tap-benefits-advisors/). The VA main information line is 1-800-698-2411.
