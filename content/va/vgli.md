## Who can get VGLI

VGLI lets you keep life insurance after you leave the military by converting your Servicemembers' Group Life Insurance (SGLI). You can apply within 1 year and 120 days after you leave service, for example after release from an active duty period of 31 days or more while covered by SGLI, or after retiring from the Ready Reserve or National Guard.

## Apply within 240 days to skip health questions

If you apply within 240 days of leaving the military, you don't need to prove good health. After 240 days, you must send evidence of good health, which can make it harder to get coverage.

## Coverage and cost

Coverage ranges from $10,000 to $500,000, depending on how much SGLI you had when you separated. You can increase coverage by $25,000 one year after getting VGLI and every 5 years after that, up to $500,000, until age 60.

Premiums depend only on your age and coverage amount. VA's page lists current rates. For a limited time, VA announced a premium holiday: eligible VGLI policyholders owe no premiums from November 1, 2026 through January 31, 2027 (details: https://www.benefits.va.gov/insurance/vgli_premium_holiday_faqs.asp).

## How to apply

Apply online through the Office of Servicemembers' Group Life Insurance (OSGLI), run by Prudential, or by mail or fax with the Application for Veterans' Group Life Insurance (SGLV 8714, https://www.benefits.va.gov/INSURANCE/forms/sglv-8714.pdf). You can change beneficiaries online or with form SGLV 8721.
